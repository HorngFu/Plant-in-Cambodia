# Code of Conduct

Plant in Cambodia is a shared, public project. Everyone who takes part (editors, photographers,
developers, people who open issues) is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
  Khmer and English are both welcome everywhere in this project.
- Give credit: cite your sources and name the people whose work you build on.
- Disagree about facts with evidence, not personal remarks.
- Protect nature: never share exact locations of threatened or poached species.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's personal information without their permission.
- Copying text or photos you don't have the right to share.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers by contacting
[@HorngFu on GitHub](https://github.com/HorngFu). Reports are handled in confidence.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or block people who break this code of conduct.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
